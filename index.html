IEEE published, 2025

SI-FF-ArcNet: identical twin face recognition

A hybrid of Swin Transformer and InceptionV4 that fuses features at several scales and trains with ArcFace loss to produce 512-D face embeddings. Recognition is open-set: cosine similarity decides whether a face matches anyone the model knows.

  • 99.96%validation accuracy
  • 98.20%F1-score over 50 epochs
  • 512-DArcFace embedding
  • 5-foldcross-validation

Python, PyTorch, Swin Transformer, InceptionV4, ArcFace loss, OpenCV

August 2025

Real-time semaphore detection

Reads semaphore signals from body pose in a live camera feed. MediaPipe finds the pose, Flask serves the result, and the whole pipeline runs on an NVIDIA Jetson Nano so inference stays on the device.

Python, OpenCV, MediaPipe, Flask, Jetson Nano

December 2024

Movie recommendation system

A content-based recommender. Movie descriptions become TF-IDF vectors, and cosine similarity ranks the closest matches.

  • 85%recommendation accuracy
